# Switch

Switch is a native checkbox exposed with switch semantics. Use it for a setting that takes effect immediately, such as turning notifications on or off.

<h2 id="playground">Playground</h2>

Toggle the setting in the preview.

<SwitchPlayground />

<h2 id="usage">Usage</h2>

Provide a clear `label` and, when useful, a `description`. Use `checked` with `onChange` for a controlled switch, or `defaultChecked` for an uncontrolled switch. The component forwards native checkbox attributes such as `name`, `value`, `disabled`, and `required`.

<h2 id="switch-guidance">Guidance</h2>

- Label the setting by the feature it controls, such as “Email notifications.”
- Keep the effect immediate and make the current state visually clear.
- Use Checkbox when the choice is submitted with a form or can be combined with other choices.
- Do not use color or position alone to communicate the on/off state.
